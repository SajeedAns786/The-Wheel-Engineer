Purpose

Built From Experience. Moving Towards the Future.

The Wheel Engineer is an extension of my family’s tyre business.

My father has been running a tyre shop for more than 15 years, and I grew up around the business. Since I was around 10 years old, I regularly visited the shop, observed the business, interacted with customers, and learned about tyres, punctures, tubes, and the everyday challenges of running a tyre shop.

Over the last three years, I have also worked as a Software Product Manager. This experience helped me understand customer problems, business operations, product development, and technology-driven solutions.

Now, I want to combine these two experiences our family’s practical tyre-industry knowledge and my experience in software and product management to build The Wheel Engineer.

ABOUT THE FOUNDER

From a Family Tyre Business to The Wheel Engineer

Sajeed Ansari Founder, The Wheel Engineer LinkedIn Profile

Sajeed grew up around his family's tyre business, where his father has been running a tyre shop for more than 15 years. His experience around the business gave him practical exposure to tyres, punctures, tubes, customers, and the challenges of running a tyre service business.

Alongside this, he has more than three years of professional experience as a Software Product Manager. He now wants to combine his family's tyre-industry experience with his software and product-management background to build The Wheel Engineer.

Why I Started The Wheel Engineer

My goal is to create a reliable tyre service ecosystem where customers can easily find the right tyre services, understand their tyre requirements, and get assistance when they need it.

I also have strong support from my parents, who have helped me understand the business and encouraged me to take it forward.
